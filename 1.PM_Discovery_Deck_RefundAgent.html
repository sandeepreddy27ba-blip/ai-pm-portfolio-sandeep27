01
How the Investigation Began
The trigger wasn't a strategy meeting — it was a single data point buried in a quarterly CSAT report. The refund category scored 2.8/5. Every other category averaged above 4.0. Something was badly broken, and nobody had named it yet.
I pulled 200 complaint tickets tagged 'refund' from the last 90 days. I expected to see complaints about money. What I found was something different — 73% of the tickets weren't about the refund amount. They were about silence. Riders had no idea if a refund was coming. They felt forgotten.
— PM VOICE · Discovery Week, March 2023
DISCOVERY TIMELINE — HOW INSIGHTS ACCUMULATED
PM Investigation Journey
6 weeks · 3 data sources · 12 stakeholder interviews · 4 rider intercepts
WEEK 1 · SIGNAL
CSAT Anomaly Detected
Quarterly CSAT report flagged refund category at 2.8/5 vs. 4.1 average across all categories. Initiated deep-dive. Pulled 200 complaint tickets manually.
SIGNAL: 2.8 CSAT vs 4.1 average
WEEK 2 · TICKET ANALYSIS
Complaint Taxonomy Reveals Root Cause
Coded 200 tickets into 8 complaint types. Overwhelming majority (73%) cited uncertainty/silence — not wrong amounts. "Didn't know if it was coming" appeared in 146 of 200 tickets.
FINDING: Pain = silence, not money
WEEK 3 · OPS INTERVIEW
Process Mapping Exposes Manual Bottleneck
Shadowed 3 operations staff for 2 days each. Documented every step in refund processing. Found 14-step manual process with 6 handoffs. Staff processing ~40 refunds/hr maximum.
FINDING: 4.2 day avg resolution time
WEEK 4 · DATA PULL
Error Rate Quantified for First Time
Finance team had never measured refund error rate. Built the first ever error tracking query. Found 12% of manually processed refunds had calculation errors requiring correction.
FINDING: 12% error rate, uncounted cost
WEEK 5 · RIDER RESEARCH
4 Rider Intercepts Reveal Retention Risk
Recruited 4 riders who had experienced refund delays in the last 6 months. 3 of 4 had reduced trip frequency after a bad refund experience. 1 had switched to ride-share entirely.
FINDING: Refund experience drives churn
WEEK 6 · SYNTHESIS
Business Case Crystallised
Connected CSAT → retention → revenue impact. Calculated that a 1-point CSAT improvement in refund category would recover ~₹2.4Cr in annual retained ridership revenue. Built the investment case.
OUTPUT: $1.84M ROI projection
